# Participant recruitment

**Status: not started. Nobody has been contacted.**

## Who
Screen for people who manage their own plans and commitments across email and calendar. Target mix in [validation-plan.md](validation-plan.md#segments-and-priority).

## Screener (5 questions, 2 minutes)
1. Which of these describes you best right now? *(job seeker mid-process / consultant or freelancer / founder / recruiter or interview coordinator / product manager / other knowledge worker / researcher / student / other)*
2. Roughly how many emails a week need a real reply from you? *(< 10 / 10–30 / 30–100 / 100+)*
3. Who manages your calendar? *(me / me and others / an assistant)*
4. Which do you use daily? *(Gmail / Outlook / Google Calendar / Outlook Calendar / WhatsApp for work / Slack / other)*
5. Do you work in product management, UX research, or for a company building AI assistants? *(yes / no)*

**Include:** Q2 ≥ 10–30, Q3 includes "me", uses email + a calendar daily.
**Exclude:** Q5 = yes (too close to the product — they critique the concept instead of reporting behaviour); close friends of the researcher for the main sample (use one for the pilot only).
**Balance:** no more than 3 people from the same company or friend group.

## Where to find them (in order of expected quality)
1. **Warm introductions** — ask 5 people you know for one introduction each to someone in a target segment. Second-degree contacts are more honest than friends.
2. **Job-seeker communities** — LinkedIn posts, alumni groups (MCA / Masai / IIT Roorkee cohort channels), placement groups. Job seekers are the priority-1 segment and often happy to talk.
3. **Freelancer and founder communities** — local startup Slack/Discord groups, co-working spaces in Delhi NCR.
4. **Recruiters** — LinkedIn outreach to agency recruiters and in-house coordinators; offer to share anonymised findings in return.

## Outreach message (edit to fit; send individually, not as a blast)
> Hi {name}, I'm researching how people keep track of plans and promises across email and calendar — things like reschedules, changed flights, or promises that slip. Would you do a 40-minute call about how you actually handle this today? I'm not selling anything, and I'll share what I learn. As thanks, {incentive}. Here's a 2-minute screener: {link}

## Incentive
Something modest and non-cash is fine for an independent study: a short writeup of the findings, or a gift voucher of a fixed amount (decide once, same for everyone, before recruiting). Record it in the validation log. Never tie the incentive to *what* they say.

## Tracking
Keep a private recruitment sheet (not in this repo): pseudonymous ID, segment, channel, status (contacted / screened / scheduled / done / declined). Target contact-to-interview conversion is unknown — plan to contact roughly 3× the number of interviews you need.
