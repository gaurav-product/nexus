# Consent template

Plain-language consent for an independent research study. **This is a template, not legal advice.** If you run this through an employer or university, use their approved form instead. In India, handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; if a participant is outside India, check their local rules.

---

**Study:** How people keep track of plans and promises across email, calendar and documents
**Researcher:** Gaurav Kumar Singh — contact: {researcher email}
**Date:** {date} **Participant ID:** {P0x}

**What this is.** I'm studying how people notice and handle things like reschedules, conflicting information and promises that slip. This is independent research for a product I'm designing. I'm not selling anything.

**What you'll do.** Depending on what you agree to below:
- a 40-minute conversation about how you handle these situations today;
- optionally, a 7-day diary where you log short notes (about a minute each) when these situations happen;
- optionally, a 25-minute session where you look at example screens with **made-up data** and tell me what you think.

**What I will not ask for.** Access to your email, calendar or files. Please **don't** forward real emails or share screenshots of your inbox. Describing what happened is enough.

**What I record.** Notes, and — only if you agree — an audio recording, used only to check my notes. Recordings are deleted within 30 days.

**How your information is used.** You're identified only by a code ({P0x}). Findings are reported in aggregate or with anonymised quotes that can't identify you, your employer or anyone you mention. Raw notes are stored privately, never in a public repository, and deleted within {period, e.g. 12 months}.

**Your choices.** Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, stop at any time, or withdraw later by emailing me, and I'll delete your data. The {incentive} is yours either way.
