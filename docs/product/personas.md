# Personas

> **All personas are hypotheses** built from the target segments in the research plan and from desk research. They are not composites of real interviews — none have been run. Names are fictional.

## P1 — Riya, job seeker mid-process (primary for MVP demo)
- 27, product analyst, interviewing with 4–6 companies at once. Gmail + Google Calendar.
- Recruiters send invites, then reschedule by email; some attach PDF invitation letters.
- **Situations she hits (hypothesised):** interview date/time conflicts across invite vs. email vs. PDF; her own commitments ("I'll send my portfolio by Friday"); recruiters' promises ("we'll confirm the panel by Wednesday"); document requests (signed NDA, ID proof).
- **What she fears:** missing an interview or looking disorganised.
- **Trust red line:** anything that emails a recruiter on her behalf.

## P2 — Arjun, independent consultant
- 34, 3–5 clients, travels twice a month.
- **Situations:** flight changes affecting client meetings; promises to clients; invoices/contracts requested vs. sent.
- **Trust red line:** anything touching client communication or money without approval.

## P3 — Meera, recruiting coordinator (secondary, possible B2B wedge)
- Coordinates 30+ interviews a week across candidate email, interviewer calendars and scheduling docs.
- **Situations:** professionally exposed to exactly the conflicts Nexus detects, at volume.
- **Why she matters:** if consumer frequency is low (A1 fails), coordinators may be where frequency is high. Logged as a pivot option, not an MVP target.

## Anti-persona — Sam, inbox-zero power user
- Already runs Superhuman, rules, and a strict task system. Low marginal value; would find a separate inbox redundant. Not our first user.
