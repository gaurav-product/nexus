# Value proposition

## For
People who coordinate with many others over email and calendar (job seekers, consultants, founders).

## Who
Lose track of where their sources disagree, what changed, and what's still open.

## Nexus is
A situation inbox — not a chatbot, not a briefing, not a second brain.

## That
Surfaces a small number of conflicts, changes, commitments and unconfirmed requests, each with the source evidence, an honest confidence level, and a suggested next step — and never acts without you.

## Unlike
- **Daily AI briefings** (Google CC, ChatGPT Pulse), which summarise and move on,
- **AI email agents** (Copilot in Outlook, Poke), which act on your behalf,
- **Memory apps** (Mem, Tana), which wait for you to ask,

## Nexus
Keeps each situation alive until you resolve it, shows exactly why it's there, and says "I'm not sure" when it isn't.

## Value proposition canvas (hypothesis)

| Customer pains | Pain relievers |
|---|---|
| Showing up at the wrong time | Cross-source conflict cards with both versions side by side |
| Acting on an outdated plan | Before → after change cards with affected items |
| Forgetting a promise | Commitment tracking with due dates; resolves on evidence |
| Not knowing if a document was sent | "Not found in connected sources" — honest, checkable |
| AI that's confidently wrong | Evidence + status on every card; conservative by default |
| Tools that act behind your back | No autonomous actions; every change is a user click, logged |

| Gains | Gain creators |
|---|---|
| Feeling in control | Human decides; Nexus explains |
| Looking organised | Catch issues before the other party does |
| Less re-reading | Only a handful of items, each self-contained |
