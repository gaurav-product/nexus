# Jobs to be done

> Hypotheses. Format: *When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome].*

## Core job
**When** information about my plans and promises is scattered across email, calendar and documents,
**I want to** be told — only when it matters — where the pieces disagree, what has changed, and what's still open,
**so I can** act on the right version and not drop anything, without re-reading everything myself.

## Job map

| Step | Job | Nexus capability |
|---|---|---|
| Notice | Know that something needs attention without scanning | Situation Inbox, conservative surfacing |
| Understand | See *why* — which sources, what they say | Insight card: What / Why / Evidence |
| Judge | Decide how much to trust it | Status: Confirmed → Needs confirmation |
| Decide | Pick the right version / mark done | Resolve with a choice, Dismiss, Snooze |
| Act | Do the follow-up | Suggested next step; user performs it |
| Close | Know it's handled and won't nag | Resolved list; reopens only on new evidence |

## Emotional & social jobs
- Feel **in control**, not managed by an AI.
- Not look **disorganised** to recruiters/clients.
- Not feel **surveilled** by a tool reading everything.

## Jobs Nexus deliberately does NOT take
- Write my emails. Manage my to-do list. Remember everything I've ever read. Talk to me.
